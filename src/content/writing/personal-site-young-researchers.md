# Why bother in the first place?

Because we, the younglings lost in academia and tech, need people to know what we are building and exploring.

Seriously though, for me a personal site should do the following:
1. Free and independent
2. Minimal overhead to update and maintain
3. Host an overview of background and work
4. Blog and study notes
5. Host interactive tools, code, and demos

# What are the options?

## School or Platform Sites?
Usually very limited on storage, rigid in formatting, and requires way too much friction whenever you migrate or switch focus.

## GitHub Pages & Modern Static Site Generators
The best long-term option: version-controlled, fast, zero hosting cost, customizable, and capable of running interactive tools and clean typography without vendor lock-in.
